# Executive Recommendations

The project converts its analytical signals into ten strategic recommendations. The recommendations are presented as a portfolio-management framework rather than as isolated chart observations.

## 1. Build a Three-Tier Portfolio Strategy

Manage New vehicles as the scale and reliability engine, Used vehicles as the affordability engine, and Honda Certified vehicles as the value bridge.

**Expected direction:** stronger portfolio mix and clearer roles across vehicle conditions.

## 2. Scale Honda Certified as the Strategic Bridge

Expand Certified inventory selectively for buyers seeking greater assurance without paying the full New-vehicle premium.

**Dashboard KPI:** 32.6K average price and 4.60 customer rating for the highlighted Certified positioning.

## 3. Intervene Before the Mature-Vehicle Risk Cliff

Trigger proactive repricing, promotion, reconditioning or exit decisions before ageing vehicles suffer deeper depreciation and weaker customer perception.

## 4. Turn Used Variety into Curated Choice

Rationalize weaker Used models and prioritize proven performers to convert broad inventory into a more useful customer choice architecture.

## 5. Reposition Hybrid Around Value & Experience

Strengthen Hybrid value communication and address interior and performance gaps instead of relying on reliability alone.

## 6. Expand Winning Hybrids Selectively

Concentrate investment on customer-validated Hybrid models rather than pursuing broad portfolio expansion.

## 7. Make MPG Transparency a Core Value Lever

Prioritize complete MPG information across the portfolio to strengthen information access and customer-perceived value.

## 8. Prioritize Mature Vehicles with Missing MPG Data

Target mature vehicles without MPG information for data completion, stronger value communication, repricing or exit decisions.

## 9. Use Customer Advocacy as an Investment Signal

Integrate review popularity and customer ratings into model prioritization and inventory-allocation decisions.

## 10. Build a Multi-Signal Vehicle Decision Engine

Combine vehicle condition, age, fuel type, MPG availability, popularity, ratings, reliability and inventory value to support scale, repricing, improvement or exit decisions.

## Strategic Outcome

The recommendations aim to move the organization from isolated vehicle metrics toward a connected portfolio decision system built around:

**Portfolio Mix → Customer Value → Ageing Risk → Inventory Allocation → Sustainable Growth**
