# Segmentation Framework — Honda Vehicle Performance, Pricing & Inventory Insights

## Methodology

Segmentation thresholds were established using distribution analysis, histogram evaluation, percentile assessment and business interpretability.

The project has two principal analytical segmentation systems.

## 1. Review Volume Category

### Purpose

Classify vehicle models by customer review volume to provide an interpretable signal of market visibility, customer engagement and model popularity.

| Category | Reviews |
|---|---:|
| Niche Model | <150 |
| Emerging Favorite | 150–749 |
| Popular Model | 750–1,999 |
| Consumer Favorite | ≥2,000 |

### Analytical use

Review-volume segmentation supports:

- Model positioning
- Customer advocacy analysis
- Review-volume comparisons
- Portfolio strategy
- Model-performance prioritization

## 2. Vehicle Age Category

### Purpose

Group vehicles by age to evaluate pricing behavior, customer perception and lifecycle maturity.

| Category | Vehicle Age |
|---|---:|
| New Model | 0–2 years |
| Recent Model | 3–5 years |
| Mature Model | 6+ years |

### Analytical use

Vehicle-age segmentation supports:

- Vehicle ageing analysis
- Depreciation analysis
- Customer-perception comparison
- Inventory positioning
- Mature-vehicle risk analysis
- Executive recommendations

## Analytical Application

The two frameworks are used together with pricing, ratings, inventory value, fuel type, drivetrain and other vehicle characteristics. The intention is not to create arbitrary groups, but to turn raw continuous or high-cardinality fields into business-readable analytical dimensions.
