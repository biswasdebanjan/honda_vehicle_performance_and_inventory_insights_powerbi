# Strategic Recommendations — Honda Vehicle Performance, Pricing & Inventory Insights

The dashboard analysis was translated into ten strategic recommendations. They are intended as portfolio-level actions derived from the observed relationships between vehicle performance, customer perception, pricing, lifecycle and inventory.

## 1. Three-Tier Portfolio Strategy

Use distinct strategic roles for New, Used and Honda Certified inventory, with each role serving a different value and customer need.

## 2. Scale Honda Certified as the Strategic Bridge

Expand Certified inventory selectively as a bridge between stronger used-vehicle value and the assurance associated with newer vehicles.

## 3. Intervene Before the Mature-Vehicle Risk Cliff

Trigger proactive repricing, promotion, reconditioning or exit decisions before ageing vehicles move deeper into depreciation and weakening customer perception.

## 4. Turn Used Variety into Curated Choice

Rationalize weaker used models and prioritize proven performers to create a more consistent customer choice architecture.

## 5. Reposition Hybrid Around Value & Experience

Strengthen hybrid communication around customer value and experience rather than relying on reliability alone.

## 6. Expand Winning Hybrids Selectively

Concentrate investment on hybrid models with stronger customer-validation signals rather than expanding the hybrid portfolio indiscriminately.

## 7. Make MPG Transparency a Core Value Lever

Prioritize complete MPG information across listings and customer touchpoints to strengthen perceived ownership value and decision confidence.

## 8. Prioritize Mature Vehicles with Missing MPG Data

Use mature-vehicle records with missing MPG information as a priority for data completion because transparency becomes more important as vehicles age and value becomes more sensitive.

## 9. Use Customer Advocacy as an Investment Signal

Integrate review popularity and customer ratings into model prioritization and inventory allocation rather than treating reviews as a separate reporting metric.

## 10. Build a Multi-Signal Vehicle Decision Engine

Combine vehicle age, fuel type, MPG availability, popularity, ratings, reliability and inventory value to determine whether a model should be scaled, protected, improved, repositioned or exited.

## Overall Decision Logic

The recommendations follow one principle:

> **Use multiple signals together rather than allowing a single KPI to determine portfolio action.**
